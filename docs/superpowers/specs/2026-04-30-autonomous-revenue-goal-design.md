# Autonomous Revenue Goal Design

## Goal

Allow the one-company OS to accept a business target such as "earn CNY 10,000 in one month" and autonomously create an operating plan that finds feasible projects, validates demand, creates offers, produces acquisition content, and queues CEO approvals for external actions.

## Operating Model

The system treats a revenue target as a company-level objective, not a chat prompt. Jarvis converts the goal into a monthly revenue plan; McGonagall defines service packages; Luna produces content and distribution drafts; Fred builds the lead pipeline; Dobby prepares customer success and FAQ assets; Percy tracks financial assumptions; Snape enforces risk gates; Neville turns each loop into reusable knowledge.

## Autonomy Levels

- A1 Read: inspect configs, platform dashboards, content metrics, knowledge base, sales pipeline, and previous artifacts.
- A2 Produce: create project candidates, scoring tables, offer drafts, outreach scripts, content drafts, lead forms, delivery plans, and daily reports.
- A3 Approval required: publishing, direct outreach, comment replies, private messages, quotes, payment confirmation, refunds, and customer commitments.

## Revenue Loop

1. Goal intake: parse monthly amount, deadline, constraints, and acceptable risk.
2. Project discovery: generate project candidates from current capabilities, market knowledge, account assets, and platform learning.
3. Opportunity scoring: score candidates by speed to cash, delivery confidence, audience fit, proof availability, risk, and expected margin.
4. Offer design: create one primary offer and two fallback offers with price, deliverables, boundary, and proof material.
5. Acquisition plan: generate content, outreach, and private-domain assets for CEO review.
6. Lead handling: classify leads, generate suggested replies, and queue follow-up actions for approval.
7. Delivery plan: prepare fulfillment SOP before selling.
8. Daily review: update progress, blockers, next actions, and expected revenue gap.

## Success Criteria

- A revenue goal produces at least three project candidates.
- The system selects one primary project with a defensible score.
- The system generates offer, content, sales, audit, finance, and delivery artifacts.
- Browser runtime shows the active revenue goal, target, current gap, selected project, and approval queue.
- No real revenue is booked and no external message is sent without CEO confirmation.
