# Private Domain Operations Playbook

Owner: Neville HR. Primary users: Fred Sales, Dobby Customer, Snape Audit.

## Positioning

Private-domain operations should convert interest into trust. The goal is not aggressive closing; it is qualification, expectation setting, and service fit.

## Lead Signals

- Asks whether the system can be deployed for their workflow.
- Asks price, delivery time, or implementation scope.
- Shares their current manual process or bottleneck.
- Requests a checklist, template, or demo.

## Reply Principles

- Answer the specific question first.
- Ask one qualifying question at a time.
- Do not promise full automation without understanding the workflow.
- Do not send payment or contract language before fit is confirmed.
- Keep sensitive personal or business data out of public notes and permanent agent memory.

## Handoff Rules

- Dobby handles first reply suggestions and FAQ consolidation.
- Fred owns lead scoring and next-step recommendation.
- McGonagall owns service package fit.
- Snape reviews any pricing, guarantee, or compliance-sensitive wording.

## Reusable Qualification Questions

- "你现在最想自动化的是获客、交付、客服，还是内部管理？"
- "这件事现在每周大概耗费你多少小时？"
- "你更需要现成模板，还是需要按你的流程定制？"

## Next Experiments

- Build a 3-level lead score: curious, problem-aware, project-ready.
- Map each level to one suggested reply and one next action.
