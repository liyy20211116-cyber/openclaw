## Tactic

Use a transparent build-in-public recovery narrative after platform rejection: state what failed, what changed, what the system learned, and what will be tested next.

## Why It May Work

The topic has real conflict and proof without needing aggressive selling. It turns a compliance failure into an operating story, which is safer than asking for comments or private messages. It also trains the audience to see the account as a live company-building log rather than a lead magnet account.

## Use When

- A post, process, or workflow produced a real mistake or platform signal.
- There is a concrete correction that can be shown without exposing private data.
- The account needs trust repair or a softer follow-up after a rejected note.

## Do Not Use When

- The post blames the platform or asks users to rescue the account.
- There is no real change after the failure.
- The CTA depends on "comment to receive", "private message me", or similar interaction-bait mechanics.

## Compliance Risk

- Risk level: medium
- Required reviewer: Snape Audit
- Guardrail: remove direct engagement bait, income promises, exaggerated automation claims, and any wording that implies the platform made a wrong decision.

## Reusable Template

Title or hook:

`第一条被下架后，我让一人公司改了一条规则`

Opening:

`昨天我发的第一条内容被下架了。不是平台的问题，是我自己的表达太像引流。`

Body structure:

1. What happened.
2. What rule was broken or risky.
3. What the company changed.
4. What the next post will test.

Call to action:

`我会继续记录这个系统怎么从错误里变强。`
