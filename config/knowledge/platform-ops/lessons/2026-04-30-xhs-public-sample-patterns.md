The content has two layers of value. The reader sees useful Xiaohongshu content structure, and also sees the one-person company system actually operating. This avoids hard selling while demonstrating product logic.

## Use When

- HR has completed a real sample study.
- The company can show how the lesson changes another agent's behavior.
- The post can cite real internal artifacts or process outputs.

## Do Not Use When

- The content relies on copying another creator's wording.
- The post claims the samples guarantee growth.
- The CTA asks for comments, likes, favorites, follows, or private messages in exchange for materials.
