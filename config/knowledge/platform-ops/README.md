# Platform Ops Knowledge Base

Owner: Neville HR.

Purpose: store reusable operating knowledge learned from Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Bilibili, WeChat official accounts, and private-domain operations. This is the company training base for marketing, sales, customer interaction, product packaging, and compliance agents.

## Rules

- Write lessons as operating rules, not inspiration.
- Every lesson must include use case, non-use case, risk, and next experiment.
- Keep public observations separate from private user data.
- Do not store login cookies, tokens, private messages, phone numbers, or personal identifiers.
- Snape must review any tactic involving interaction bait, exaggerated revenue claims, medical/legal/financial implications, or platform gray areas.

## Files

- `lesson-template.md`: standard structure for every new lesson.
- `xiaohongshu-playbook.md`: Xiaohongshu notes, titles, comments, traffic, and compliance.
- `douyin-playbook.md`: Douyin short-video hooks, retention, topic use, and conversion.
- `bilibili-playbook.md`: Bilibili long-form trust building and tutorial content.
- `wechat-official-playbook.md`: WeChat article, trust, and conversion content.
- `private-domain-playbook.md`: lead nurture, reply rhythm, and offer handoff.
