# Douyin Operations Playbook

Owner: Neville HR. Primary users: Luna Growth, Fred Sales, Snape Audit.

## Positioning

Douyin should test short, high-contrast hooks. It is better for visible transformation, fast conflict, and clear before-after storytelling than long operational explanation.

## Repeatable Patterns

### 3-second contradiction

- Use when: the content has a visible contrast, such as "one person running nine agents".
- Do not use when: the story needs long context.
- Template: "我用一个人，把公司拆成了 9 个岗位。今天先让它们做一件事。"
- Risk: avoid claiming fully autonomous operation if manual review is still required.

### Screen-recorded workflow

- Use when: showing dashboard, task board, publishing checklist, or monitoring loop.
- Do not use when: confidential customer or account data is visible.
- Template: show 2 seconds of problem, 5 seconds of system action, 3 seconds of result.

### Comment-to-next-video

- Use when: comments ask a real question.
- Do not use when: manufacturing fake demand or asking for engagement bait.
- Template: "有人问 X，我用今天的系统数据回答。"

## Department Enablement

- Snape checks exaggerated claims and sensitive data exposure.
- Dobby turns common questions into reply suggestions.
