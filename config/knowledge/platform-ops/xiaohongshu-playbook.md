# Xiaohongshu Operations Playbook

Owner: Neville HR. Primary users: Luna Growth, Snape Audit, Dobby Customer, Fred Sales.

## Current Baseline

- First experimental note was rejected for interaction-bait risk.
- Second note survived and reached roughly 500 views with low but real engagement.
- Third note is a recovery narrative and must be monitored before the next content escalation.

## Working Direction

Use real operating diaries instead of hard selling. Xiaohongshu is more suitable for "I am building this in public" than "buy my system now".

## Repeatable Patterns

### Build-in-public diary

- Use when: the company has a real event, metric, failure, rejection, or operational decision.
- Do not use when: there is no concrete event.
- Template: "今天一人公司发生了 X，我原本以为 Y，结果 Z，所以我改了 A。"
- Owner: Luna.
- Review: Snape checks exaggerated claims and interaction bait.

### Failure-to-learning post

- Use when: content was rejected, data was weak, or a process broke.
- Do not use when: the post blames the platform or asks users to rescue the account.
- Template: "第一版错在 X，第二版我改成 Y，学到的是 Z。"
- Owner: Luna plus Snape.

### Operational proof, not income proof

- Use when: showing agent division, dashboard, workflow, or checklist.
- Do not use when: implying guaranteed income, wage payment, or financial promise.
- Template: "我不是在晒结果，我在记录这个系统怎么分工。"
- Owner: Luna plus Fred.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Avoid "评论区领取", "私信我发你", "点赞收藏再看" as primary mechanics.
- Avoid hard income promises and exaggerated automation claims.
- Avoid presenting AI employees as real employees with payroll, labor, or employment claims unless clearly framed as a metaphor.
- If a note has sales intent, Snape must approve before publishing.

## Next Experiments

- Compare "failure diary" vs "workflow screenshot" as the next two notes.
- Track views, likes, favorites, shares, comments, private messages, and qualified leads.
- A note is considered reusable if it beats the previous surviving note on save/share ratio or produces one qualified lead.

## 2026-04-30 Neville Day 1 Rule Additions

### Rejection becomes training material

- If a note is rejected, Neville must convert it into a prevention rule within 24 hours.
- Luna receives the writing lesson.
- Snape receives the compliance pattern.
- Jarvis receives the workflow rule.

### No sales escalation without intent

- Views, likes, favorites, and shares are attention signals, not lead signals.
- Fred should only escalate when a user asks about implementation, workflow fit, cost, timeline, or service scope.

### Recovery narrative guardrail

- A recovery post should take responsibility for the risky wording.
- It should not blame the platform.
- It should name the process improvement.
- It should avoid asking readers to comment or private message for materials.

## 2026-04-30 Public Sample Learning Rule

### Learn structure, not content

- Neville may study public Xiaohongshu search results for structure learning.
- The company should not copy note bodies, images, or another creator's exact positioning.
- The reusable learning unit is: title shape, audience angle, proof signal, save reason, and risk.

### Content angle created from learning itself

- HR learning can become content when it changes the company workflow.
- The post should show what Neville learned and which agent was trained.
- This is stronger than saying "we studied爆款", because it proves the one-person company is operating.
