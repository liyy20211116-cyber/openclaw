# WeChat Official Account Playbook

Owner: Neville HR. Primary users: Luna Growth, McGonagall Product, Fred Sales.

## Positioning

WeChat official account should store deeper trust content: operating memos, long-form cases, service explanations, and decision records. It is the most suitable public archive for serious buyers.

## Repeatable Patterns

### Operating memo

- Use when: a real decision was made after data review.
- Do not use when: the content is just a short daily update.
- Structure: situation, data, decision, trade-off, next action.

### Case narrative

- Use when: a customer-like scenario can be explained without private data.
- Do not use when: it implies guaranteed revenue.
- Structure: background, pain, one-company workflow, result, limitation.

### Offer explainer

- Use when: Fred or McGonagall needs a buyer-ready page.
- Do not use when: the offer is not yet validated by real lead questions.
- Structure: who it is for, problem, deliverables, process, boundary, price logic.

## Department Enablement

- Luna writes the article.
- McGonagall packages the offer.
- Fred extracts sales follow-up wording.
- Snape audits claims, privacy, and pricing language.
